# Evidence Card

Use evidence cards to prevent vibe-based recommendations. Keep them compact.

## Format

```markdown
### [Pain cluster]
- **User segment:** who has the pain
- **Pain statement:** one specific sentence
- **Evidence quotes:** 2-5 short quotes with source/date/platform
- **Source breadth:** platforms + independent source count
- **Existing workaround:** what users do today
- **Paid-solution evidence:** paid apps/SaaS/services, pricing, or explicit WTP
- **Competitor context:** direct/adjacent alternatives
- **Confidence:** High / Medium / Low
- **Why it matters:** concrete impact, not generic annoyance
```

## Confidence

- **High:** 3+ independent sources, paid alternatives or explicit WTP, recent evidence, clear user segment.
- **Medium:** 2 independent sources, plausible WTP, some competitor evidence.
- **Low:** 1 source, vague pain, unclear buyer, or mostly speculative discussion.

## Rules

- Quote real user language when possible.
- App Store / marketplace reviews count as strong evidence because they come from users of existing solutions.
- Separate complaints about a product bug from unmet market demand.
- Do not inflate one viral thread into multiple independent sources.
- If evidence is weak, mark it weak and recommend validation, not building.
